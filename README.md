# Groceries

A personal grocery list web app for iPhone.

## How to use
- **Add:** type in the bar at the bottom and press return. The keyboard stays open for the next item.
- **Suggestions:** things you've bought before appear above the keyboard. Tap one to add it, or hold it to remove it from suggestions.
- **Tick off:** tap an item. Ticked items sink to the bottom of their section.
- **Clear:** tap **Clear** (top right) to remove all ticked items. You can undo this for 5 seconds.
- **Wrong section?** Hold an item, then pick the right section. The app remembers it next time. You can also delete the item from this menu.

Items are sorted automatically from Dutch, English and Chinese words.

## Add it to your iPhone
1. Open that link in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen → Add**.
3. Open it from the new icon. From then on it works offline too.

Your list is saved on your phone, inside the home-screen app. Deleting the icon also deletes the list.
